# VZG Vision

PROJECT:

VZG CONSULT — Premium B2B Expert Brand Website

MISSION:

Build a production-quality German-first website for VZG CONSULT.

This website is NOT a generic AI agency website and NOT the HORIZON platform.

VZG CONSULT is the personal expert and commercial B2B brand of Vilislav Gitsov — founder, builder and the person taking direct responsibility for every project.

The positioning is:

VZG CONSULT

AI Systems · Automation · Digital Products

Brand philosophy:

ONE VISION. ONE BUILDER. FULL RESPONSIBILITY.

The website must communicate that one highly involved founder takes a project from business idea through process analysis, architecture, AI/automation design and implementation.

Do not pretend VZG CONSULT has a large team.

Do not invent employees, customers, awards, certifications, testimonials, revenue or project results.
